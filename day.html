The Day

A day by the sea — from the afternoon sun into the small hours.

Programme

Order of the day

14:30 – 15:00

We arrive!

Make yourself at home on the shores of Castricum aan Zee. Find a familiar face, kick off your shoes and let the afternoon begin.

15:00 – 16:00

We do!

The main event. We say I do with our feet in the sand and the North Sea as our backdrop — exactly where we always imagined it would be.

16:00 – 16:30

We toast!

Pop the bubbly and raise a glass. A moment to celebrate, take a breath and let it fully sink in — we're actually married.

16:30 – 18:00

We borrel!

Dutch borrel style — cake, drinks and good vibes on the terrace. Mingle in the golden hour as the afternoon turns warm and the sea sparkles.

18:00 – 20:30

We eat!

Straight from the BBQ. We're on the beach, so we're doing it properly — a proper feast with sea breeze, great wine and the people we love most.

20:30 – late

We celebrate!

Speeches, surprises and then the dance floor opens. We'll keep going until the staff gently suggest it might be time to head home.

Dress code

Boho chic

Think boho chic. We're right on the beach, so keep it comfortable — and wear something you can dance in.
